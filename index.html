    Beyond the Blackhat Label

    Veteran-Founded, Same Standards

    Tanto Vodka isn't distilled at Blackhat's facility — it's a private-label partnership, produced and bottled by Blackhat Distillery under founders Ben Morgan and Kris "Tanto" Paronto's own brand.

    Both are former members of the elite 75th Ranger Regiment, and 50% of all Tanto profits support veterans and first responders — a smaller part of the catalog, but one that runs through the same Blackhat quality standards.

    — Blackhat Distillery, on the Private Label line
